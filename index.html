Questions, answered

Before you apply.

Do I need gym experience?

No. HJKfitness is designed especially for beginners and women returning after time away. Your starting point determines the program.

Do I need access to a commercial gym?

No. Your plan can be built around a commercial gym, apartment gym, or the equipment you currently have access to.

Is nutrition included?

Yes. Coaching includes flexible nutrition guidance, food-journaling support, and feedback based on your goals and preferences.

How quickly will I hear back?

After applying, Jasmine or Iris will contact you within 72 hours to learn more about your goals and determine whether coaching is the right fit.

What happens after I join?

You will complete onboarding, receive app access, schedule your onboarding call, and begin with a personalized plan built from your intake information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need gym experience to apply?

Not at all. My coaching is designed specifically for women who are new to the gym or looking for guidance. No prior experience is required.

Do you only coach women?

Yes. My 1:1 coaching is exclusively for women, and I'm not currently accepting male clients.

How long will it take to hear back after I apply?

Please allow up to 72 hours after submitting your application. You'll hear from either me or my assistant, Iris, with the next steps.

Who is Iris?

Iris is my assistant. She reviews applications, answers questions, and helps determine whether my coaching is the right fit before scheduling your onboarding call with me.

What does it mean to “qualify”?

It simply means we're making sure my coaching is the right fit for your goals. If we believe another resource or approach would serve you better, we'll always be honest and point you in that direction. I'd rather work with women I know I can genuinely help than accept someone into a program that isn't the best fit.

Cancellations & Refunds

If you cancel before your coaching start date, you are eligible for a full refund of any amount paid.

Once your coaching start date has passed, cancellations are subject to an early termination fee equal to 50% of the remaining balance on your program. Your remaining balance is calculated as your monthly coaching rate multiplied by the number of months left in your program at the time of cancellation.

For example: if your monthly rate is $400 and you enroll in a 4-month program, cancelling after your 2nd month leaves 2 months remaining ($800). You would owe 50% of that remaining balance, or $400.

Billing. Coaching fees are billed monthly, beginning on the date of your first payment. Your billing date does not change based on your program start date. It recurs on the same date each month as your original payment.

Access After Cancellation. If you cancel after your coaching start date, your access continues through the end of your current paid billing period (one month from your most recent payment date), after which your access will conclude.

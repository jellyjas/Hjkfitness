When you apply for coaching through this site, we collect the information you provide in the application form, including your name, email address, phone number, Instagram handle (optional), age range, fitness goals, time zone, and other details you share about your experience and preferences.

We use this information to evaluate your application, communicate with you about your application status, and, if you're accepted, to provide coaching services. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Sharing Your Information

Your information may be shared with HJKfitness team members, such as our assistant Iris, solely for the purpose of reviewing applications and providing coaching services. We do not share your information with outside parties for marketing purposes.

Your Rights

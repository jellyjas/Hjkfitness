How It Works

A simple, honest process — no pressure, just making sure it's the right fit before we start.

01

Apply

Fill out a short application — about 2 minutes. It covers your goals, your experience level, and what's held you back before, so I can understand where you're starting from.

02

Review

Either I or my assistant, Iris, review every application within 72 hours. We're checking that this is genuinely the right fit for your goals — not just filling a spot.

03

Onboarding call

If it's a good fit, we'll schedule a call to walk through how coaching works, answer your questions, and make sure you feel confident before we begin.

04

Start coaching

Once you're in, we get to work — building a plan around your life, your goals, and where you're actually starting from.

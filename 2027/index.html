Before you ask

I've never really lifted. Can I do this?

That's exactly who it's for. Every workout is written for someone new to the gym.

Do I need a gym membership?

Yes. The workouts use common gym equipment like the leg press, cable machines, Smith machine and dumbbells, so have a membership lined up by December 1.

What if I miss a day?

You pick it back up the next day. Missing a day doesn't end anything. Stopping does, and that's what the group and the check-ins are there for.

What if I can't do an exercise?

Every exercise has an approved swap, so you're never stuck if a machine is taken or a move doesn't feel right. If something causes sharp or unusual pain, stop and message me.
